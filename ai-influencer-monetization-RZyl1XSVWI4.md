## Proposed implications for Creator Studio
Retain the association between character, audience, product, landing page, content/CTA, checkout and fulfillment. Test actual delivery before launch. Separate organic and paid traffic, revenue and costs. Keep approval gates for product quality, persona disclosures, claims, publishing and spending. Do not present fictional lived financial success as genuine expertise.
